# Arcora Pricing & Plans

Arcora is built on a private, local-first architecture designed to keep your household admin organized, secure, and always under your control.

## Basic Plan
- **Price:** $0 / Free Forever
- **Features:** Full access to all 6 core dashboard modules:
  - **Bills & Finance:** Track utility due dates, payment cycles, statements, and payment histories.
  - **Subscriptions:** Monitor active plans, renewal intervals, projected costs, and cancellation metrics.
  - **Warranties:** Log product coverage, purchase receipts, serial numbers, and check expirations.
  - **Documents:** Secure personal vault for contract agreements, passports, tax forms, and medical records.
  - **Passwords:** Encrypted credential safe for home Wi-Fi keys, utility logins, and landlord portals.
  - **Reminders:** Schedule custom priorities, priority levels, and recurring domestic maintenance alerts.
- **Privacy Model:** Client-side AES-256 local session encryption (no server-side records access).
- **Data Portability:** Full data backup, import, and export capabilities.
