NADRA PakID is an online portal (mobile app) for filing applications for various types of Pakistani Identity cards. Through PakID, you can apply for:

  • Smart National ID Card for Overseas Pakistanis (Smart NICOP): Fresh – Renewal – Modification – Reprint/Lost – Update of Non-PrintableField

    Click for more details

  • Smart ID: Renewal – Modification – Reprint lost - Update of Non-Printable Field

    NOTE: First time/fresh processing of Smart ID is not available at NADRA PakID.
    Click for more details

  • Smart Pakistan Origin Card (Smart POC) - Fresh – Renewal – Modification

    Click for more details

  • Child Registration Certificate -(CRC) - Issuance of Fresh CRC for children up to one-year age

    Click for more details

  • It’s fast. Apply hassle free in few simple and easy steps.

  • It’s convenient. Apply for your identity card from anywhere in the world, at PakID mobile application (available on Android & IOS) from the convenience of your home or office.

  • Delivery Worldwide. Whether you live in Pakistan or abroad, your card will be delivered to your doorstep, whereas Certificates (CRC, FRC and Cancellation) will be sent to your PakID account inbox.

The PakID Mobile Application allows you to easily acquire your Identity card in a few simple steps:

  • STEP 1: Registration

    Register your account on PakID Mobile Application (available on Android and iOS ).

  • STEP 2: Capture/Upload Photograph

    Upload or Capture the photograph

  • STEP 3: Provide Required Details

    Provide all the required information in each respective section like Application details, Personal details, Relative details, Address details, Card delivery information.

  • STEP 4: Upload Fingerprints & Required Documents

    Upload the required documents and fingerprints form. The fingerprints need to have a 600-dpi scanning quality or you can capture the fingerprints through PakID mobile app in real time (Check this Guide about Fingerprints verification on Mobile App )

  • STEP 5 & 6: Make Payments & Submit Application

    For applications related to Smart ID modification, Family Registration Certificate and Cancellation Certificate, you must first submit the application. After the approval by NADRA, you will be notified through an email to pay the fee, which is the last step. For all other applications, you must pay the fee before submitting the application.

    • Note: Payment can be made through Credit & Debit cards (MasterCard or Visa). Moreover, Smart ID and FRC application fees can also be paid through e-Sahulat franchise, Easy paisa and Jazz cash.

  2. Press the setting icons on the right top.

  6. Your account will be permanently deleted.

Follow these steps for the successful capturing of the fingerprints.

  1. Fingerprints tips must be aligned with the boxes.
  2. Use a plain background or a white paper while fingerprints capturing.
  3. Capture the fingerprints in well-lit environment.
  4. Keep the hand/fingers stationary by resting it on fixed or solid surface.
  5. There must be appropriate gap between the fingers.
  6. While capturing the fingerprints, the hand should not be too far or too close to the camera.
  7. Don’t tilt or curl the fingers.
Note:Applicant is allowed 10 attempts to capture fingerprints through the PakID mobile app. In case of failure, the applicant needs to provide the fingerprints on the verifier form. You can download a blank verifier form through this link.

Yes, you can upload the photo of your choice by clicking the browse button and select the photograph on your mobile. The photograph must not be older than 6 months. Or alternatively, you can capture the photograph on the mobile app by clicking on the capture button. Once the photograph is clicked, it would be saved automatically and then you can proceed further by clicking the continue button.

NOTE:

  • Capture the photo against a plain or white background.
  • Avoid the use of hats, artificial lenses, glasses, or anything that obscures the face.
  • Ensure there are no shadows on the face, no hair across your eyes, and no obstructions of the face view.
  • Applicant should have a straight posture and must be looking straight at the camera.
  • Avoid wearing uniform or colors matching the background.
  • Photograph must not be older than 6 months.
  • Black and white photograph is not acceptable. Make sure that photograph is taken with appropriate brightness and contrast.
  • Female applicant can capture/upload the photograph in hijab/abaya after ensuring the clear visibility of forehead, complete face including chin.
  • Male applicant is allowed to capture/upload the photograph in headgear (cap, turban) after ensuring the clear visibility of complete face.
  • Photograph must be original without filters or layers and has not been altered in any way.

"DPI" stands for "dots per inch," a measurement of resolution in scanning. 600 DPI means 600 dots or pixels scanned per inch. Following are the steps for scanning the form at 600 dpi.

  1. Place the fingerprints form on the scanner.
  2. Press the scan button on the scanner.
  3. Following screen will appear
  4. Click ok to proceed further.
  5. The following window will appear next
  6. To scan the form, select the following parameters:
    1. Select Grayscale from Color format drop down box.
    2. Select JPG (JPG File) from File type drop down box.
    3. Select 600 dpi in Resolution (DPI) box.
  7. Then click scan button.
  8. Once the scanning is completed, following window will appear to save the scanned file.
  9. Click Import button to save your scanned document at default printer location.
  10. Once the file is saved, the system automatically opens and displays the scanned file location.
  11. Now the file is ready to be uploaded to PakID web application.

You will receive your identity card at your address, through courier service.

  1. For Inland applicants:

    The courier will contact the applicant at provided contact number for delivery, once the confirmation is received from the applicant; courier delivers the identity card at the doorstep of the applicant. In case, the applicant is not reachable, the courier tries to contact the applicant for 3 days, after that the card is delivered to the nearest NADRA office.

  2. For Overseas applicants:

    The courier will contact the applicant before the delivery of the card, once the confirmation is received from the applicant, the card is delivered at the door step of the applicant. In case the applicant is not reachable, the courier, after the lapse of a specific period of time, delivers the card to the nearest Pakistan embassy/mission.
