# Pakistan Sample Pack

This folder contains public reference files from official Pakistani or Pakistan-market sources that can help you test document categories before launch.

Important notes:
- These are mostly public reference pages, blank forms, or support pages.
- Public real-world customer samples for subscriptions, bank statements, and many warranty cards are rare because they usually contain private customer data.
- For parser testing, the bill files here are the most directly useful public references.
- For subscriptions and warranties, these files are better treated as structure/reference material than perfect OCR test samples.

## What exists in Pakistan

### Documents
- CNIC / Smart ID
- NICOP / POC
- Passport
- Child Registration Certificate (CRC / B-Form)
- Family Registration Certificate (FRC)
- Driving licence
- Bank statement
- Account maintenance certificate
- Salary slip
- Tax / FBR documents
- Vehicle registration and ownership papers
- Educational certificates and transcripts
- Health / lab reports

### Bills
- Electricity bills
  - K-Electric
  - DISCO bills such as LESCO, FESCO, IESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, QESCO
- Gas bills
  - SNGPL
  - SSGC
- Internet / landline bills
  - PTCL
  - Nayatel
  - StormFiber and other ISPs
- Mobile postpaid bills
  - Ufone
  - Jazz
  - Zong

### Subscriptions
- Broadband / ISP monthly invoices
- Mobile postpaid monthly invoices
- Streaming invoices
- Insurance premium reminders
- Club / gym / education / software recurring invoices

### Warranties
- Home appliance warranty cards
- Mobile phone warranty cards
- Battery / UPS / inverter warranty cards
- Solar inverter / solar panel warranty documents
- Vehicle warranty booklets
